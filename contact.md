We extend an invitation to groups and individuals who share our desire for
peace with justice in Israel/Palestine.

If you are interested in having Christian-Jewish Allies present a program in
your community, or if you would like to join our work, please contact us at
[info@cjallies.org](mailto:info@cjallies.org).

If you are also working for peace with justice in Israel/Palestine, please let
us know about your work.

We won't fill your inbox with unwanted email, and we won't share your name
without asking your permission first.

We would love to hear from you.
