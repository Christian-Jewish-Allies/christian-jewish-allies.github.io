title: "A Brief History"
permalink: /history/
---

Christian-Jewish Allies grew out of local participation in the Tree of Life
Conference in Philadelphia in November 2009. The founding of CJAllies reflects
our shared sense of urgency as Jews and as Christians to strategically work
together toward justice and peace in the Holy Land.

Our members identify as Baptist, Catholic, Episcopalian, Jewish, Quaker,
Lutheran, Mennonite, Presbyterian, United Methodist, United Church of Christ,
Orthodox, Sufi, and non-denominational.

Since our founding, our interfaith teams have presented at numerous workshops
and denominational gatherings, and we have taught Steadfast Hope adult
education courses at several regional churches. We have sponsored film
screenings and hosted a variety of visiting speakers. CJAllies has formed
important relationships with local and national organizations, and we are
regularly invited to plan and present programs in church communities throughout
the Delaware Valley.
