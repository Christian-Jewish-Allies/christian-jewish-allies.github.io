All of our work is rooted in collaborative Christian-Jewish interfaith
partnerships. Teams of Jewish and Christian colleagues are involved in
everything we do.

CJAllies offers a variety of programs and resources on Israel/Palestine,
tailored to the particular needs of each faith-based community:

- Steadfast Hope and Zionism Unsettled adult education courses
- One-time presentations and educational workshops
- Film screenings and facilitated discussions
- Reading-group resources, with or without facilitation
- Hosting internationally-known speakers, including religious leaders, scholars,
  peace activists, filmmakers, and journalists
- Offering fair-trade Palestinian products, such as olive oil and crafts, for
  resale
- Resources for groups planning a delegation to the Middle East
